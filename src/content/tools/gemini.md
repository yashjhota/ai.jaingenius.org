## Getting started

1. Sign in with your Google account on the official website, or open the Gemini app.
2. Paste a prompt from our [Prompt Library](/prompts). Image prompts work here too.
3. Fill in the details in [SQUARE BRACKETS] and send it.
4. Ask for changes, or say "try again" for a new version.

## Tips for Jain content

- For posters, ask for no text in the image and add your words afterwards in a design app. AI images often misspell words.
- Check generated images carefully for anything inappropriate, such as wrong symbols or figures.
- Research answers can contain mistakes. Check religious facts and dates with a trusted source.
