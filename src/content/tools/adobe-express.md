## Getting started

1. Open the official website and create a free account.
2. Start a new design in the size you need, such as an Instagram post or a story.
3. Upload one of our [backgrounds or borders](/assets) and place it behind your design.
4. Add your text with the text tool, then download it as PNG or PDF.

## Tips for Jain content

- Keep religious imagery dignified. Use approved images from your temple or organisation where possible.
- Check that stock images and stickers suit the occasion before using them.
- Check spelling of names, dates and venues carefully before you print or share.
