## Getting started

1. Go to the official website and choose **Sign up for free** (or install the official app).
2. Paste a prompt from our [Prompt Library](/prompts) into the message box.
3. Replace anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS] with your own details and send it.
4. Ask for changes in plain words, such as "make it shorter" or "write it in Hindi".

## Tips for Jain content

- Ask it to keep to facts that are widely accepted across Jain traditions, and to say when something differs between traditions.
- Always check religious names, dates and quotations with a trusted source. Chat assistants can sound confident and still be wrong.
- Do not share private details of other people in your chats.
