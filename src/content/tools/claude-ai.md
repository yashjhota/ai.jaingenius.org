## Getting started

1. Go to the official website and sign up for a free account.
2. Paste a prompt from our [Prompt Library](/prompts), or describe what you need in plain words.
3. Fill in the details in [SQUARE BRACKETS] and send it.
4. Ask for changes until you are happy with the draft.
