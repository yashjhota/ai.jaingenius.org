## Getting started

1. Open the official website or the Google Translate app.
2. Choose the language you are translating from and the language you want.
3. Type or paste your text. The translation appears straight away.

## Tips for Jain content

- Machine translation often gets religious and formal words wrong. Ask a fluent speaker to check anything you will print or share widely.
- Keep well-known terms as they are. For example, "Jai Jinendra" and "Michhami Dukkadam" should not be translated.
- Translate short, simple sentences for better results.
