Help me plan a presentation for a Jain youth group.

- Topic: [TOPIC, e.g. "Jain values at college and work"]
- Audience: [e.g. 18 to 25 year olds]
- Length: [e.g. 20 minutes]
- Number of slides: [10 to 15]

For each slide, give:

1. Slide title.
2. Three to five short bullet points.
3. A visual idea (photo, icon or simple chart), avoiding images of Tirthankaras or deities unless I supply an approved image.
4. Speaker notes of 2 to 4 sentences.

Include an opening slide with a question to engage the audience, and a closing slide with one practical takeaway and a discussion question.

Keep facts general and widely accepted. Mark any statement that needs checking with "[CHECK]". Do not invent quotations or statistics.
