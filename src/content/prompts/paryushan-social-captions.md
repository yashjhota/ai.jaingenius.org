You are writing social media posts for [ORGANISATION OR YOUTH GROUP NAME] during Paryushan Parva.

Write [NUMBER, e.g. 8] short captions, one for each day of Paryushan, for Instagram and Facebook.

Each caption should:

- Be 2 to 4 short lines.
- Focus on one value, such as forgiveness (kshama), self-reflection, simplicity, non-violence (ahimsa), self-control or kindness.
- Suggest one small, practical action readers can take that day.
- Be positive and respectful, suitable for all ages.

For the final day, write a caption about asking for and offering forgiveness, ending with "Michhami Dukkadam".

After each caption, suggest 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.

Do not invent scripture quotations, stories or historical facts.
