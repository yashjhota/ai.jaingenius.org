Explain the Jain concept of [CONCEPT, e.g. "anekantavada (many viewpoints)" or "aparigraha (non-attachment)"] to children aged [AGE RANGE, e.g. 7 to 10].

Please include:

1. A one-sentence meaning in simple words.
2. A short, original everyday story (school, home or playground) that shows the idea. Make it clear the story is made up for teaching.
3. Three examples of how a child can practise it this week.
4. Two discussion questions a teacher can ask the class.

Requirements:

- Language: [ENGLISH / HINDI / GUJARATI].
- Use short sentences and words children know.
- Only use explanations widely accepted across Jain traditions. Do not invent quotations, scripture references or historical stories.
- Add a final note: "Teachers: please check this explanation with a trusted source before use."
