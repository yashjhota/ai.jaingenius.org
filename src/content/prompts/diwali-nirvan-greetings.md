Write 5 short Diwali greeting messages from a Jain family to friends and relatives.

Context: for Jains, Diwali marks the Nirvan (moksha) of Bhagwan Mahavir. The messages should reflect this, along with light, inner peace and good wishes for the new year.

Requirements:

- Each message should be 2 to 3 lines long, suitable for WhatsApp.
- Language: [ENGLISH / HINDI / GUJARATI].
- Sign off with: [YOUR FAMILY OR ORGANISATION NAME].
- Keep the tone warm and simple. Do not include firecrackers.
- Do not mention other religions' deities or rituals.
- Do not invent scripture quotations. If you include a well-known phrase such as "Jai Jinendra", use it exactly.
