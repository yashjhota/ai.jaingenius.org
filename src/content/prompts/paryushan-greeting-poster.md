Create a serene and elegant greeting poster for Paryushan Parva, the Jain festival of forgiveness and self-reflection.

Style: minimal and dignified, with a warm cream background, deep maroon and soft gold accents, and a subtle border inspired by traditional Jain manuscript painting.

Main visual: a single softly lit lotus in the centre, with gentle light rays behind it.

Layout: leave clear empty space at the top for the heading "Michhami Dukkadam" and at the bottom for a family or organisation name.

Avoid: human figures, deities, religious figures, extra text, watermarks and cluttered decoration.

Aspect ratio 4:5, suitable for Instagram and WhatsApp.
