You are helping a Jain pathshala teacher prepare a quiz.

Create 10 multiple-choice questions for children aged 8 to 12 on this topic: [TOPIC, for example "the five main vows (vratas)"].

For each question give:

1. The question, in simple English.
2. Four answer options labelled A to D.
3. The correct answer.
4. A one-line explanation a child can understand.

Rules:

- Only include facts that are widely accepted across Jain traditions. If a fact differs between Shvetambar and Digambar traditions, leave it out or say clearly which tradition it applies to.
- Do not invent quotations, scripture references or stories.
- Start the quiz with the line: "Draft — please check against a trusted source before use."
