Write a script for a [30 / 45 / 60]-second Instagram reel for a Jain youth group.

Theme: simple ways to practise ahimsa (non-violence) in everyday life, in thought, word and action.

Format the script as a table with these columns: Time (seconds), What we see on screen, On-screen text, Voice-over.

Requirements:

- Open with a hook in the first 3 seconds.
- Show 4 or 5 everyday examples, such as speaking kindly, not wasting water or food, being careful not to harm insects, and choosing kind words online.
- Use filming ideas a group can shoot on a phone without special equipment.
- End with a short call to action and the group name: [GROUP NAME].
- Suggest background music mood (not a specific copyrighted song).

Keep it positive and practical. Do not invent quotations from scriptures or acharyas.
