A clear invitation layout for pujas, pravachans, shibirs, pratishtha mahotsavs and community gatherings. Pair it with the [event invitation prompt](/prompts/temple-event-invitation) to draft the wording.

## How to edit

1. Download the SVG file.
2. Open it in [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org) (free), Figma or Adobe Illustrator.
3. Double-click each piece of text in [square brackets] and type your own words.
4. Export as PNG or PDF to share or print.

**Using Canva?** Canva does not keep SVG text editable. Upload the blank [festive background](/assets/festive-background) or [lotus border frame](/assets/lotus-border-frame) instead, and add your text with Canva's text tool.
